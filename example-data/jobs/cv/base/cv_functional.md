## Core Competencies

### Platform engineering

- **Kestrel Financial (Senior Data Platform Engineer):** owned the data platform serving 60+ analysts and engineers — Snowflake, dbt, Airflow and a Kafka ingest path — through a period when the company roughly doubled in size, without a corresponding increase in platform headcount.
- **Kestrel Financial:** migrated a monolithic nightly batch to incremental dbt models, taking the critical path from **6 hours to 35 minutes** and making same-day reporting possible for the first time.
- **Meridian Retail Group (Data Engineer):** built the event pipeline behind the customer-analytics product, moving ~40M events a day from a hand-rolled ingest script to a managed streaming setup.

### Reliability & cost

- **Kestrel Financial:** introduced SLOs on the twelve pipelines that actually had downstream consumers, and retired alerting on the thirty that didn't — on-call pages dropped by roughly two thirds over three quarters.
- **Kestrel Financial:** cut warehouse spend **40%** with no measurable drop in query throughput, mostly by fixing clustering and killing the long tail of unused scheduled queries.
- **Kestrel Financial:** built the lineage view that made "what breaks if I change this?" answerable — the first internal tool colleagues asked for by name.

### Enablement

- **Kestrel Financial:** designed and ran the analytics-engineering enablement programme — 40+ analysts, from SQL-only to owning their own dbt models and reviews. Measured by models shipped without platform-team involvement, which went from zero to the majority within a year.
- **Kestrel Financial:** wrote the internal data documentation that people actually cite in reviews, replacing a wiki nobody trusted.
- **Meridian Retail Group:** paired with the sales-engineering team on three enterprise onboardings, translating between the customer's data model and ours.

### Community & writing

- Maintains **dbt-audit-helper-lite**, a small open-source dbt package (~400 GitHub stars) for lightweight model auditing.
- Four conference and meetup talks, two of them keynotes at regional data meetups, on pipeline reliability and on teaching analytics engineering.
- Writes a data-engineering blog with a small but genuine readership; two posts have been cited in vendor documentation.
