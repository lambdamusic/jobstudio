# Study — Grafana plugin architecture

Written after the HR screen, where the study flag was raised. Notes to have in mind for
the hiring-manager round, not answers to recite.

## Panel, data source, app

Three plugin kinds, and knowing which is which is the whole vocabulary: a **panel**
renders data someone else fetched, a **data source** is the thing that fetches, and an
**app** bundles both with its own pages.

## What to say

The observability workshop series shipped dashboards, not plugins — be clear about that
line rather than implying depth that isn't there.
