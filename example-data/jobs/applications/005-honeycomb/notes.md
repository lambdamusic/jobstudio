# Honeycomb — Developer Advocate

## My notes

### Outcome

Rejected 2026-08-05, and worth recording why rather than just the status. Their feedback
was specific and generous: the writing was strong, the production experience was there,
and the gap was cadence — they wanted someone already speaking roughly monthly, and the
count here is two keynotes in eighteen months. They said to reapply.

### What that changes

- The switch needs more public surface area before the next role like this, not a better
  CV for it. That is a six-month problem, not a rewrite.
- The two-keynotes framing was doing too much work in the summary. It reads as a lot
  until someone asks how often.

### Preparation steps, for next time

- Get the talk count up: the local meetup circuit is the cheap route.
- Keep the SLO story — it was the strongest part of the conversation and the part they
  came back to twice.
