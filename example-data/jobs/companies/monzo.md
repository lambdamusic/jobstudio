# Monzo

## Mission

UK digital bank. Consumer-facing, app-first, with an engineering reputation built on a
microservices platform and unusually open engineering writing.

## Technology

Go microservices, Kubernetes, Kafka as the backbone. The data platform sits on
BigQuery. Their engineering blog is genuinely detailed — the data-platform posts are
worth reading before any interview.

## Where they are now

London-centred with some remote roles. Regulated environment, which changes the shape
of data work: lineage and auditability stop being nice-to-haves.

## Fit with my mission

★★★☆☆ — closest to the current job, which is both the appeal and the problem. Fintech
again, platform again, and the regulated-data angle would make the lineage work
genuinely valuable rather than a side project. But it's the "can do" option rather than
the "want to do" one, and it is the least aligned with the advocacy direction.
