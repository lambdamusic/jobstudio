# Grafana Labs

## Mission

Open-source observability: dashboards, metrics, logs and traces, built around Grafana
itself and the LGTM stack (Loki, Grafana, Tempo, Mimir). The commercial product is
Grafana Cloud; the open-source project is the reason anyone has heard of them.

## Technology

Go and TypeScript in the core, with a large plugin ecosystem. Prometheus-native.
Everything meaningful is public on GitHub, which makes the engineering culture unusually
easy to assess before applying — read the RFCs.

## Where they are now

Remote-first with a strong UK presence. Hiring across both platform engineering and
developer relations, which is exactly the overlap this search is aimed at.

## Fit with my mission

★★★★☆ — the strongest match of the three. Observability is adjacent to the pipeline
reliability work at Kestrel, and Grafana has a real developer-advocacy function rather
than a marketing team with a different job title. The open-source package and the
speaking history are directly relevant here in a way they wouldn't be at a
platform-as-cost-centre employer.

Open question: whether the DevRel roles want a Go engineer who speaks, or a speaker who
can read Go.
