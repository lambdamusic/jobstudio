# dbt Labs

## Mission

The company behind dbt, the transformation tool that turned analytics work into
software engineering — version control, tests, and code review for the warehouse.
Commercial product is dbt Cloud.

## Technology

Python core, Jinja-and-SQL as the user-facing surface. The interesting part is less the
technology than the practice it popularised — analytics engineering exists as a job
title largely because of this company.

## Where they are now

Remote-friendly with UK hiring. Competition from open-source forks and from warehouse
vendors building the same features natively is the live strategic question.

## Fit with my mission

★★★★☆ — the enablement programme at Kestrel was, in effect, teaching dbt for a year,
and dbt-audit-helper-lite is in their ecosystem already. Closest thing to "the thing I
already do, done full time".

Reservation worth noting: being a heavy user of a tool is not the same as wanting to
build it, and the roles here lean toward the latter.
