# Target areas

Working notes on the two directions this search is aimed at. The structured
version of each lives in `jobs/targets/*.yaml`, which is what the CV generator and
the portal scan actually read; this file is for the thinking around them.

## Data Platform & Infrastructure

The obvious continuation. Eight years of platform work, and the market for it is
deep. The risk is doing the same job at a different logo — so the filter is whether
the platform is the *product* (Grafana, dbt Labs) or a cost centre supporting
something else.

## Developer Advocacy & Technical Enablement

The one that's actually interesting. The enablement programme was the most
satisfying work of the last three years, and the blog and the dbt package suggest
this is where the energy is. Less certain, fewer roles, and a pay question to
resolve — but "can do" versus "want to do" points here.
