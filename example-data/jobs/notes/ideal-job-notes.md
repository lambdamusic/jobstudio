# What the next role should look like

Rough, unstructured, and updated whenever something becomes clearer. The structured
version lives in `jobs/profile/criteria.yaml`; this is the thinking behind it.

## The honest version

Eight years of platform work means the platform roles are easy to get and the advocacy
roles are not. The temptation is to take the easy one and tell myself the enablement
work will happen on the side. It didn't at Kestrel until it was made someone's actual
job — mine — and that took two years of asking.

## What I'm actually optimising for

- **Technical users.** The enablement programme worked because the audience could read
  code. Roles where the audience is non-technical are a different job.
- **A product I'd use anyway.** Both of the strong fits are tools already in the stack.
- **Somewhere the writing counts.** Four talks and a blog are either the beginning of a
  track record or an expensive hobby, and which one depends on the employer.

## What I'll trade

Title, mostly. A sideways move into advocacy is fine. Salary within about 10% is fine.

## What I won't

Going back to being the only platform person. Done twice, and the second time was worse
than the first.
